### Rewrite 1: complete story pass

**Room:** Sara greets the captain in person. “I'm Sara, a Director at the Merchant Corporation. We trade oil, lumber, and gold across space. Welcome to Vela. Your skills will come in handy.”

**Island inserts:** “We've found Farreach, an island far from our base. No one has explored it. We think it has oil, gold, and wood. It can be beautiful. It can also be dangerous. Storms sweep over the coast. We don't know what lives in the forest.”

**Assignment:** “Explore it, find what is there, and report back. If there are useful resources, build a base and collect them. You'll lead a crew and keep ten percent of the profit.” Explain profit. Ask whether captain accepts; No →15%, No again →20% final. Silent waiting loops between replies.

**Team/departure:** Sara introduces Rho; brief handshake. Rho walks alongside captain with a map. “We'll check the coast, find water, and choose a place to land.” Aircraft rolls down runway, lifts, crosses sea. Clear island wide; no hands.

**Storm/crash:** Clouds rapidly cross flight path. Rho says to turn back. Captain turns. Gust damages engine; aircraft loses height. Rho points to blue escape handle. Captain pulls it; crew escape under parachutes. Empty ship crashes beside the beach. Wind scatters parachutes.

**Beach:** Rho finds captain at dawn. Crew scattered; radio cannot reach base. “Find the crew. Build a camp. Save the ship's parts. We'll repair it.” Rho explains that captains listen, plan and learn. Future goal: build a base, trade across planets, lead a space empire. First task: search wreck for food and tools. Plain learning disclosure sits outside the cinematic.
