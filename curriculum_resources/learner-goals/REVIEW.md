# REVIEW: ambiguous standards (G3–4 learnerGoals)

- **ELA.3.F.1.3** — "Use phonics and word parts to read new words" — REVIEW: Description bundles four decoding sub-skills (phonics, roots/affixes, derivational suffixes + parts of speech, multisyllabic); goal can only summarize.
- **ELA.4.F.1.3** — "Use letter sounds, syllables, and word parts to read" — REVIEW: Covers both reading AND writing unfamiliar words via letter-sounds, syllables, morphology; goal emphasizes reading.
- **MA.3.AR.1.1** — "Use multiplication properties to find products" — REVIEW: Two skills (distributive property for 1x2-digit, plus multiplication properties); goal generalizes to 'multiplication properties'.
- **SS.3.A.1.3** — "Define important social studies words" — REVIEW: 'Define terms related to the social sciences' is very vague; no specific term list given.
- **SS.4.A.1.2** — "Combine Florida history information from print and online media" — REVIEW: 'Synthesize information' is abstract for kids; paraphrased as 'combine'.
- **SC.4.N.2.1** — "Explain that science studies only the natural world" — REVIEW: 'Science focuses solely on the natural world' is a nature-of-science idea that may need teacher framing.
